DBQuizzes

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

DBQuizzes is a YouTube channel. Its videos are made and uploaded by a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer. This policy explains what that tool does with data.

Who uses the tool

Only the owner of the DBQuizzes channel. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What Google data it uses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own quiz videos and their thumbnails.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's own uploads, so the same video is never uploaded twice.

The tool does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How it is stored

The sign-in token is saved only on the channel owner's computer, in a file only that account can read. It is never sent to anyone else, sold or shared.

Viewers

Watching DBQuizzes videos on YouTube is covered by Google's privacy policy. DBQuizzes does not collect personal information from viewers.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact details on the DBQuizzes YouTube channel.
